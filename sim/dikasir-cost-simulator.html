Which assumption actually moves the answer

Each knob taken to its low and high plausible value, everything else held. Bars show the swing in final-year EBITDA against the current setting.

Read it this way. Retention and churn are ordinary product decisions that move the result by miliar. Peak traffic — the thing infrastructure work usually optimises — barely registers. Spend the engineering week on keeping merchants, not on right-sizing instances.

What this model is honest about

  • Prices are real. Every rate comes from the AWS Price List Bulk API for ap-southeast-3 on 2026-09-07 — Multi-AZ PostgreSQL at $1.079/hr for db.r6g.xlarge, gp3 at $0.276/GB-mo, egress at $0.132/GB, CloudWatch ingest at $0.70/GB.
  • The capacity constants are not measured. Requests absorbed per vCPU, write rows per second and bytes per order are engineering estimates. Load testing is still open work on this project, so read the infrastructure figure as right to within about 2×, not 10%.
  • Write amplification is taken from the code. One checkout writes about eleven rows — Order, three OrderLine, a Payment, three InventoryMovement and three InventoryStock updates, plus an AuditLog on corrections — in a single transaction.
  • No private pricing is assumed. List rates throughout, with reserved-instance coverage the business has not yet committed to. An enterprise discount at year-3 spend would take real money off the top.
